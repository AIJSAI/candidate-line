Under "TwiML™️ Voice: <Stream>":
> <Start><Stream> creates a unidirectional Stream. <Connect><Stream> creates a bidirectional Stream.
> When you use <Connect><Stream>, Twilio doesn't execute subsequent TwiML instructions.

Under "Attributes":
> On successful execution, Twilio establishes a wss WebSocket connection to the URL and begins streaming audio. wss is the only supported protocol.
> The url does not support query string parameters.

Under "Stop a Stream":
> Note: The only way you can stop a bidirectional Stream (one started with <Connect><Stream>) is to end the call.

DR-005 also records that the page as read states no limit of one Stream per call; the nearest text, under
"Attributes", is about the name attribute:
> This name must be unique per Call.
