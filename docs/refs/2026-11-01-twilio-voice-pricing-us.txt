Voice pricing FAQs
How does Twilio Voice pricing compare to other vendors?

When you use Twilio for your voice communications, you get what you pay for: best-in-class documentation and SDKs, a gold-standard developer experience, reliable global carrier relationships for exceptional connectivity, and a full platform that brings all your communications together under one roof.
We’ve invested in foundational elements that make your life easier and future-proof your solutions like compliance certifications (HIPAA, GDPR, PCI, SOC 2, ISO/IEC 27001) to meet high security and compliance standards. As well as AI capabilities to power smart transcription, sentiment analysis, and other data points to capture key data about customers every time you interact with them.
You can find vendors who charge less upfront, but when you build with Twilio, you get a premium offering that works reliably and delivers lasting ROI over time.

Are there any hidden fees?

No, there are no hidden fees with Twilio. We make our pricing transparent and only charge you for your usage so there are no billing surprises. You can also scale up or down at any time based on your call volumes and budget.
When do volume discounts kick in?

You automatically unlock volume discounts when you hit a threshold of monthly voice minutes. These discounts apply when you move into the next threshold for voice minutes.  For example:
	•	First 100,000 minutes: standard rate
	•	Next 900,000 minutes: lower rate
	•	Next 9 million minutes: lower rate
Volume pricing applies to U.S. and Canadian voice minutes, messages and phone numbers.

Are there extra fees for features like call recording, transcription, and Voice AI agents?

Yes, there are fees for additional Twilio Voice features.
	•	Call recording costs $0.0025 per minute for recording. (Scroll up to the Intelligence service pricing section for more details.)
	•	Voice AI features have additional charges, such as running virtual agents with Google Dialogflow CX, virtual agent custom prompt voices, and text-to-speech. (Scroll up to the Intelligence service pricing section for more details.)
