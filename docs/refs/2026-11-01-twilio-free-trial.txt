Under "Use additional verified recipients":
> You can call or message only verified recipients from a trial account. Twilio removes this security restriction when you upgrade your account.

Under "Use your Twilio credentials for the API":
> Your Twilio trial account uses the same account credentials (Account SID and Auth Token) that you use after you upgrade. There are no separate trial-only credentials.

Under "Trial account restrictions and limitations":
> 30-day expiration: Trial ends 30 days after sign-up.
> Verified recipients: You can only send to verified phone numbers (up to 5 per account).
