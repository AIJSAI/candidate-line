# Prompt card 08: the outbound screening call

Work item: the line calls an applicant back within minutes of a signed application with consent, asks the role's posted questions, and either books an interview (every posted requirement met) or hands the application to a recruiter with a promised call (one not met), with the gate in code and a person making every hiring decision.
Record: `docs/decisions/DR-025-outbound-screening.md` (DRAFT). Its Contracts section is what every lane builds to; spec section 9 holds the lines each piece serves (AGENTS.md rule 2). Lane 0 wrote the spec lines, the record, this card, the refs in `docs/refs/` and the data in `data/`; the proposed eval cases wait outside `evals/` (below).

### Lane B: intake and the dialler

Files: `candidate_line/outbound/__init__.py`, `candidate_line/outbound/intake.py`, `candidate_line/outbound/contacts.py`, `candidate_line/outbound/window.py`, `candidate_line/outbound/dialler.py` (all new), `candidate_line/telephony/twilio_rest.py` (new: the probe's standard-library client moved here and extended), `candidate_line/telephony/app.py`, `candidate_line/telephony/media.py`, `candidate_line/settings.py`, `candidate_line/probe/dial.py` (its answer mode and the shared client), and their tests: `tests/test_intake.py`, `tests/test_contacts.py`, `tests/test_window.py`, `tests/test_dialler.py`, `tests/test_twilio_rest.py` (new), `tests/test_webhook.py`, `tests/test_media.py`, `tests/test_settings.py`, `tests/test_probe_dial.py`.

Acceptance criteria:
- Intake refuses, each in its own test: no key (503), a bad signature, a missing or malformed nonce, a stale timestamp, a nonce seen in the last ten minutes (409), including one seen before a restart and found on a stored application, an extra key, a bad number, zone, PIN or role, an unknown consent version, a body over 4 KB. A request whose signature covers the timestamp and the body but not the nonce is refused. A good one answers 202 with the code and never the number or the PIN; no consent, an opted-out number, or a number not on OUTBOUND_ALLOWED_NUMBERS (status `not_allowed`) is stored with its reason in `not_dialled`, writes no raw number and no dial, and is never dialled (fix 13, fix 14).
- The allow-list, with a fake Twilio: a due dial whose contact number is not on OUTBOUND_ALLOWED_NUMBERS is never placed even when every other gate holds (window, cap, consent, no opt-out, no kill file), it is cancelled with outcome `not_allowed` and one masked log line; a number on the list is placed; `/voice` answers `<Hangup/>` to an outbound call whose `To` is not on the list (fix 13).
- The window and the attempts, on a pinned clock: inside and outside the window in four US zones, no Sunday time ever returned (a Saturday 19:59 miss moves to Monday), the window's edges, attempt 2 at plus two hours moved into the window, attempt 3 the next calling day at the window's middle, none past MAX_DIAL_ATTEMPTS.
- The dialler, with a fake Twilio and a pinned clock, places nothing while OUTBOUND_ENABLED is off, `var/kill` or `var/no-dial` exists, the task is under 120 s old, the day's cap is reached, a call is in flight, the applicant has no consent or opted out, or the Polly preflight answers AccessDenied (which also sets `var/no-dial`); and otherwise places one call with exactly the parameters of DR-025 section 12 (fix 8).
- `/voice` on an outbound call: no dial record answers `<Hangup/>`; a record found by CallSid or by the signed dial id skips the inbound count, starts the call with channel `outbound` and the masked `To` (fix 7), and answers `<Say>` with the opening before `<Connect><Stream>` with the token. `/voice/status` and `/voice/amd` refuse a bad signature, schedule the next attempt on no answer, busy or failed, and leave the voicemail on the first attempt's machine only.
- The media session on an outbound call: the PIN ask at stream start, hello words never barge in or reach the model before the PIN matches (fix 2), the 480 s cap (fix 1), `on_call_end` and the dial's outcome off the event loop (fix 5).
- The contacts store: mode 600, the number readable only through `Contacts.number`, written only for an application with a dial scheduled; `purge` deletes a number on the first dialler tick after CONTACTS_RETENTION_DAYS, with OUTBOUND_ENABLED on and off; the same tick runs `Ledger.purge_answers` for ANSWERS_RETENTION_DAYS; `delete` removes a contact at once on an opt-out or a deletion request (fix 16); a test greps every span, log line, post and cockpit page of a full scripted outbound call for the raw test number and finds none.
- Each new setting refused at start when zero or nonsense, `MAX_CONCURRENT_OUTBOUND` other than 1 refused, outbound on with a missing key, allow-list, account id or line number refused, an allow-list with an empty or non-E.164 entry refused, a CALL_WINDOW that names Sunday refused; the defaults are MAX_OUTBOUND_PER_DAY 20, MAX_DIAL_ATTEMPTS 3, CONTACTS_RETENTION_DAYS 30 and ANSWERS_RETENTION_DAYS 1460, each held by a test (fix 16, fix 17).
- The opening, tested for every fixture applicant: it names the applicant's first name and none of the role's title, site or recruiter, the application code or any answer (Never 5, fix 17).
- The probe's answer mode: it posts a signed synthetic application for its own number, answers the line's call with one short hello, plays a met case and a not-met case, records dual-channel and deletes the recording; run live only with James watching.
- `uv run pytest -q` green; ruff clean.

Counsel's read before any real applicant's number is dialled: the consent text in `data/applications.yaml`, whether a recruiting call counts as a solicitation under the federal and state hour rules, whether 47 CFR 64.1200(b)(2) is met by saying the line's own number, the Illinois notice, whether the gate's yes or no is simplified output under New York City's rules (then a bias audit, or the no-gate fallback), and California's automated-decision regulations. Until then OUTBOUND_ALLOWED_NUMBERS holds only the probe's number and the operator's, and lifting it is a decision with its own record, never a setting changed alone.
3. Money: the 45 USD budget stop, which 10 screens a day reach on about day 25 of a month (DR-025, Spend); MAX_OUTBOUND_PER_DAY (20 by default); the Twilio balance's own top-up limit, the one stop on Twilio's side.
4. Retention: the defaults are CONTACTS_RETENTION_DAYS 30 and ANSWERS_RETENTION_DAYS 1460 (four years, California's minimum for automated-decision data, DR-025 Evidence); whether he wants them different, how long the consent records outlive the answers, and who handles a deletion request.
5. The calling window (09:00 to 20:00, Monday to Saturday, the applicant's zone; never Sunday, in the contract) and whether federal holidays need skipping too; three attempts with one voicemail on the first; the applicant's first name in a voicemail a household member may hear.
6. Who gives the applicant their PIN and code (the careers form's thank-you page and email, proposed), and what a rotation of INTAKE_SIGNING_KEY does to stored PIN hashes (re-key, or ask the applicant to call in).
7. The recruiter's access: the cockpit is the operator's alone through the SSM port-forward, so a recruiter needs a login (a stack change) or the hand-off email's link reaches only the operator.
8. Accommodation and language: whether the opening offers a person in place of the questions (one line, to `request_callback`), and whether a Spanish line is wanted.
9. Whether Gus reads the answers back for correction before the outcome line, and whether "within one business day" is the promised time.
